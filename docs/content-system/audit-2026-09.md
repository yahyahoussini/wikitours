## 6. Hajj 1448 timeline

Re-searched the Ministry of Habous. **The timeline is unchanged and already correctly
recorded** in `data/official-facts.json` — nothing newly published:

- Electronic registration: **8–19 December 2025**
- Lottery (القرعة): **2–13 March 2026**
- Quota: 34,000 — 22,400 ministry, 11,600 licensed agencies
- Initial lump sum for those drawn: 65,000 MAD

The facts file even carries the right instruction: *« the dates are those of the 1448
season and change every year — prose says "une fenêtre annoncée chaque année" and never
a date »*. No re-anchoring was required.

**What this means strategically, and it matters more than the dates.** Both gates of the
1448 cycle closed months ago. From today until the Hajj itself (May 2027), no Moroccan
can still register for 1448. So Hajj content between now and then must not read as
"how to sign up" for this season — and the queued `inscription-hajj-maroc-les-etapes`
(22 September) was checked line by line for exactly this. It passes: it frames the Hajj
as « un cycle annuel », says registration happens « pendant une fenêtre annoncée chaque
année », and positions itself as preparing the reader « avant la prochaine fenêtre ».
That is correct and it contains no date. The gate could not have caught a temporal error
here — it forbids dates in prose, so a post can be *temporally* wrong without containing
one — and this one simply is not wrong.

The live commercial opportunity is the other side: everyone who lost the March 2026
lottery is, right now, the audience for the rejected→Omra bridge. `HajjBridgeCTA` and
`pas-tire-au-sort-hajj-omra` already exist and the calendar keeps 12 Hajj slots in P5
around Arafat. The next registration window (1449) would be expected around December
2026 by the pattern, but **it has not been announced, so no date is stated anywhere** —
and none should be until Habous publishes it.

## What was fixed at system level

Three fixes, each at the rule rather than the content. Isolated build green after all three:
306 static pages generated · SEO suite 282 pages (249 indexable), 0 failures · schema
78/78 · unit tests 87/87.

### 1. The drift re-gate — `npm run content:gate -- --existing`

The root cause behind §1, §2 and §4. Rows already in `articles` were never re-checked, so
a rule added after a row was written had no reach over it. `scripts/content-gate.mjs`
gains `--existing[=all|scheduled|published]`, which runs all seventeen rules over table
rows instead of draft files.

Its exit code is deliberately asymmetric, and the reasoning matters more than the code:

- a **scheduled** row that fails **exits 1** — it has not gone out, so it is still a
  decision someone can take;
- a **published** row that fails is listed as **backlog and exits 0** — it is history, and
  a permanently red gate is exactly the pressure that gets gates loosened. The rulebook
  forbids loosening this one.

It also exposed a false red of its own, now fixed: a scheduled row has no prerendered
page in a normal build — that *is* publish-by-time working — so G13 and G16 used to fail
it for being absent. They now detect a `CONTENT_PREVIEW_SCHEDULED` build from the output
itself and report `pending` rather than a failure when there isn't one.

Results today: **all 28 published rows fail** (backlog). **8 of 10 scheduled rows fail**;
the 2 written to the contract pass.

### 2. Hijri anchors are now a constraint — `scripts/build-content-calendar.mjs`

`hijri_anchor` was decoration. It now sets a real deadline: an anchored topic must be
placed at least `lead_days` (7, new in the spec) plus `tolerance_days` (2, already there)
before its event, so it is live and findable in time. Ordering was made deadline-aware in
the same pass, so an anchored topic is picked *earlier* rather than dropped.

One deliberate exception, added after the first attempt dropped two topics that were not
wrong: **an explicit `earliest` outranks the anchor**, because a few topics are *about*
what follows their anchor — the homecoming after the Hajj, preparing for the next season —
and say so with a window that opens after it.

Cost and result: **7 misplaced slots → 0**, for one slot of capacity (169 filled / 74
unfillable, from 170 / 73). One topic, `seasonal-coupure-mi-annee`, is now honestly
unplaceable — no free slot exists before its Sha'ban deadline — which is the intended
behaviour: the file's own rule is that a slot no topic can fill is recorded with its
reason rather than filled by loosening something. All four `scheduled`/`skipped` slots
came through the rebuild byte-identical.

### 3. Sitemap `lastmod` no longer predates publication — `src/app/sitemap.js`

Blog `lastmod` was `updated_at ?? published_at`. Because every post is *written ahead* of
its slot and *released* when `published_at` passes, `updated_at` is routinely older than
the day the URL first answered 200 — true for **17 of 28** live posts, by up to 13 days.
A brand-new page entered the sitemap back-dated by two weeks. It is now the later of the
two, via the existing `lastModifiedOf()` helper. Verified on the rebuilt sitemap:
28/28 correct as instants, **0 now earlier than publication**.
